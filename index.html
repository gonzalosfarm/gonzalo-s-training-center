About Us

Gonzalos Training Center is an agricultural training institution committed to developing practical knowledge and skills in farming and agricultural production. The center promotes sustainable, productive, and environmentally responsible agricultural practices through hands-on learning and skills development.


Through practical training and farm-based activities, Gonzalos Training Center aims to help trainees develop competencies that can be applied in actual agricultural enterprises and livelihood opportunities.

Training Programs

Agricultural Crop Production

Training in basic and practical crop production practices including land preparation, planting, crop care, harvesting, and post-harvest handling.

Sustainable Farming Practices

Introduction to environmentally responsible farming practices, proper resource utilization, soil management, and sustainable agricultural production.

Farm Management

Practical knowledge in farm planning, record keeping, farm operations, resource management, and basic agricultural enterprise management.

Organic Agriculture

Learning about organic production principles, natural farm inputs, soil fertility management, and environmentally friendly farming methods.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical, and accessible agricultural training that develops competent, productive, and responsible individuals capable of applying sustainable farming practices.

Our Vision

To become a trusted agricultural training center that contributes to skilled farmers, sustainable agricultural enterprises, and stronger rural communities.

Our Objectives

• Develop practical agricultural skills.
• Promote sustainable farming practices.
• Prepare trainees for agricultural livelihood and employment opportunities.

Farm Practices

Crop Production

Soil Management

Proper soil preparation, fertility management, and conservation practices.

Organic Inputs

Proper utilization of organic materials and sustainable farm inputs.
